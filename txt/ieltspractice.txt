Part 3: Reading Comprehension (10 questions)
Read the passage below and answer the questions.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming various sectors of the economy, from healthcare to finance. Machine learning algorithms can analyze vast amounts of data to identify patterns and make predictions that were previously impossible. In healthcare, AI assists in diagnosing diseases more accurately and quickly than human doctors alone. Financial institutions use AI for fraud detection and risk assessment. However, the rapid advancement of AI also raises concerns about job displacement and ethical considerations. Critics argue that without proper regulation, AI could exacerbate inequality and privacy issues. Proponents counter that AI will create new job opportunities and enhance human capabilities rather than replace them entirely. The debate continues as society grapples with balancing innovation and responsibility in the age of artificial intelligence.
