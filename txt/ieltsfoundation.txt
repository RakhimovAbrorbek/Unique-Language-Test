Part 3: Reading Comprehension (10 questions)
Read the passage below and answer the questions.

Climate change represents one of the most significant challenges facing humanity in the 21st century. Rising global temperatures, caused primarily by greenhouse gas emissions, lead to melting ice caps, rising sea levels, and extreme weather events. Scientists agree that immediate action is necessary to prevent catastrophic consequences. Renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind power, offer promising alternatives to fossil fuels. Additionally, individuals can contribute by reducing energy consumption, supporting sustainable practices, and advocating for policy changes. The transition to a low-carbon economy requires collective effort from governments, businesses, and citizens worldwide.
